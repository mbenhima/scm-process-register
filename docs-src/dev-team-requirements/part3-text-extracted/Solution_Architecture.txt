A macro-process (D01) decomposes into tasks and steps (D02); each step may trigger a business rule (D03) executing an action (D03a); actions are governed by controls (D04) and mitigate risks (D05); outcomes are measured by KPIs (D06) and surfaced via alerts (D07) and reports (D08) built on a canonical data model (D09/D10). That same process backbone drives the integration surface, AI use cases and requirements (D11-D20), governs how it's delivered, adopted and licensed (D21-D30), and — added in this revision to close gaps against Functional Specification v1.3 — is scheduled, gated and infrastructure-hardened by the D32 series.
